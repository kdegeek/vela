# Vela liquid-glass concept review

The requested sequence is complete: **two Astra reviews**, **one Fable review**,
then **GPT-6.1 Sol mockups**. These are design artifacts, not a production UI
change or native rendering qualification. Physical Shield testing remains paused.

- [Open the interactive prototype](mocks/index.html): one recommended direction,
  26 selectable concept states, Night/Day and Glass/Opaque comparisons.
- [Representative PNG exports](mocks/renders/): actual HTML-rendered concepts.
- [Mockup handoff and verification](mocks/README.md), [asset provenance](mocks/ASSETS.md).
- [Coordinator synthesis](sol-mockup-brief.md): decisions, factual qualifications
  and product continuity, including the superseded flat/no-glass direction.
- Independent reviews: [Astra art direction](astra-art-direction.md),
  [Astra experience](astra-experience.md), [Fable critique](fable-design-critique.md).

The reviewers converged on reducing utility chrome, improving artwork hierarchy,
preserving clear remote focus, contextual Ask, a grouped Undo receipt and
person-first management. The recommended concept uses warm cinematic glass with
crisp artwork, restrained edge light and equally considered opaque surfaces.
The synthesis preserves useful disagreement and corrects overclaims about
first-run Undo, scrolled Settings and platform rendering constraints.

The coordinator independently rendered representative states and reproduced all
**72 local prototype checks** after correcting a material-toggle return path.
Approval copy was aligned to a real guarded action. The full prototype was shown
inline in the parent, with concept labels and interaction proposals kept clear.
The Android sources and production settings were untouched by this exploration;
their earlier test/build evidence remains separate.

Next implementation decisions are the owner's visual feedback, the final material
and typography choices, and later native rendering/focus/performance validation.
No implementation or device sign-off is implied by the mocks.
